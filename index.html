FAQ

Questions, answered

Can’t find what you need? Message us on WhatsApp.

What happens at my first visit?

A friendly chat about your goals, a full check-up with digital imaging, and a clear explanation of your options. No treatment starts without your agreement.

Is treatment painful?

Comfort comes first. We use gentle techniques and modern anaesthetics, and you can pause at any time.

Do you treat children and families?

Yes — we welcome patients of all ages in a calm, child-friendly environment.

Can I get a cost estimate before treatment?

Always. You’ll receive a transparent written plan before anything begins.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the form below, call us, or message on WhatsApp — we’ll confirm a time quickly.
