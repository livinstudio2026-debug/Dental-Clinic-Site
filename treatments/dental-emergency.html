About Dental Emergency & Pain Relief

Dental pain rarely waits. We prioritise urgent cases — toothache, swelling, broken or knocked-out teeth — to relieve pain and protect your tooth.

Timings and visits are typical and vary by person — we confirm everything at your consultation.

What counts as an emergency?

Severe pain, swelling, bleeding, or a broken or knocked-out tooth.

What if a tooth is knocked out?

Keep it moist, handle it by the crown, and call us immediately.
