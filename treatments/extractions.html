About Extractions & Wisdom Teeth

Sometimes a tooth can’t be saved, or a wisdom tooth causes pain and infection. We plan every extraction carefully, keep you comfortable and guide your recovery.

Timings and visits are typical and vary by person — we confirm everything at your consultation.
