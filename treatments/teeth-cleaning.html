About Teeth Cleaning (Scaling & Polishing)

Even careful brushing misses hardened plaque (tartar). A professional scale and polish removes it gently, lifts surface stains and leaves your teeth smooth and fresh.

Timings and visits are typical and vary by person — we confirm everything at your consultation.

FAQ

Good to know

More questions? Message us on WhatsApp.

Does scaling loosen teeth?

No. It removes deposits that damage gums; healthy teeth stay firmly in place.

Is some sensitivity normal?

Mild sensitivity for a day or two can happen and settles quickly.
